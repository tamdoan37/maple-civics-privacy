Frequently asked questions

Is this the official IRCC test?

No. Maple Civics is an independent study aid built on the official guide, Discover Canada. IRCC doesn't publish its question list. Always check the current rules at canada.ca.

How do I switch between English and French?

Settings → Language. Questions, answers and voices switch too.

Can Listen Mode play with the screen locked?

Yes. Start Listen Mode, then lock your phone: it keeps playing, and you can pause it from the lock screen. Leaving Listen Mode stops the audio.

A name looks out of date (premier, MP…)

Names change after elections and appointments. We update them in new versions of the app; please email us if you spot one.

How do I start over?

Settings → Reset app data. This clears your progress and setup on this device.

Does the app collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads or analytics. See the Privacy Policy.
