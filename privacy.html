Privacy Policy — Maple Civics

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Maple Civics ("the app") is a free study aid for the Canadian citizenship test. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains — in plain language — what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no trackers, and no analytics. Everything you do stays on your device.

What is stored, and where

The app saves the following only on your device (using the operating system's local storage). It is never uploaded to us or anyone else:

  • Your first name and province or territory (from the setup screen)
  • Your study progress, quiz history, and your answer preferences
  • Your settings (reminder preferences, language, voice choice)
  • If you use "Find My MP": your riding and your MP's name (never your postal code)

Because this data lives only on your device, we cannot see it, and it is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): If you turn on the daily study reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. These reminders are generated on your device and are not sent through us or any server.

The app does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, location, or photos. Sharing from the Support screen sends only a text message through the phone's share sheet.

Listen Mode

Listen Mode plays voice recordings that are built into the app. They play offline. Nothing you study is sent anywhere to make audio. When no recording exists (for example a live name such as the premier or your MP), the phone's own text-to-speech reads the text on the device.

Exam Mode

Exam Mode is a practice simulation of test day. The app does not use the camera or the microphone. The "photo check" is only a simulation, and nothing is recorded.

Network use

  • Find My MP (optional): Canada has no free postal‑code table the app can carry offline, so when you type your postal code the app sends it once to Represent by Open North, a free non‑profit civic lookup, to find your federal riding. The app does not store the postal code, and it sends nothing else (no name, no device ID). If you'd rather not use the network, pick your riding from the built‑in list instead. Onboarding works out your province from the first letter of your postal code without any network.
  • Officials list: The app may download a small public JSON file of current officials (for example, the Prime Minister and premiers) so names stay current. This sends none of your data.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Since no data is collected at all, it is safe for all ages.

Third parties

We do not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or any third‑party service that would receive your information. The only outside service is the optional Represent postal‑code lookup described above.

  • Ratings and reviews: if you choose to rate the app, the rating prompt is shown by Apple or Google, and anything you submit goes to them under their own privacy policies.
  • Buy Me a Coffee (Android only): the optional “Buy me a coffee” button opens the Buy Me a Coffee website in your browser. The app sends nothing to it. Any payment you make there is handled by Buy Me a Coffee under its own privacy policy.

Not affiliated with the government

Maple Civics is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) or the Government of Canada. Always confirm current test information at canada.ca/citizenship-test.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
