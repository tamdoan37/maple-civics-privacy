Privacy Policy — Maple Civics Last updated: November 10, 2026 Maple Civics ("the app") is a free study aid for the Canadian citizenship test. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains — in plain language — what the app does and does not do with your information. The short version The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no trackers, and no analytics. Everything you do stays on your device. The only exception is the optional "Find My MP" lookup described below. What is stored, and where The app saves the following only on your device (using the operating system's local storage). It is never uploaded to us or anyone else: Your first name and province or territory (from the setup screen) Your study progress, quiz history, and your answer preferences Your settings (language, voice, reminder preferences) If you use "Find My MP," your riding and your MP's name (never your postal code) Because this data lives only on your device, we cannot see it, and it is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data (Settings → Reset app data). Permissions Notifications (optional): If you turn on the daily study reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. These reminders are generated on your device and are not sent through us or any server. Audio: Listen Mode plays voice recordings built into the app, and keeps playing when the screen is locked. If no recording exists, your device's built-in speech engine is used. No audio is recorded and nothing is sent anywhere. The app does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, location, or photos. Network use The app works offline, except for "Find My MP." When you type your postal code there, the app sends it once to Represent by Open North (represent.opennorth.ca), a free non-profit civic lookup, to find your federal riding. Nothing else is sent, and the postal code is not saved. You can pick your riding from the built-in list instead. The app may also download a small public JSON file of current government officials (for example, the Prime Minister and premiers) so names stay current. This is an ordinary download of public information and sends none of your data. Children's privacy The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Since no data is collected at all, it is safe for all ages. Third parties We do not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or any third-party service that would receive your information. The only outside service is the optional Find My MP lookup described above. We do not sell or share any data. Not affiliated with the government Maple Civics is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with or endorsed by IRCC or the Government of Canada. Always confirm current test information at canada.ca/citizenship-test. Changes to this policy If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
