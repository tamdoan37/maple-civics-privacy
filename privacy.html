Privacy Policy — Maple Civics

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Maple Civics ("the app") is a free study aid for the Canadian citizenship test. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information. It applies to the app on iPhone, iPad and Android.

The short version

The app does not collect, sell or share personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no trackers and no analytics. What you do in the app stays on your device. The one exception is optional: Find My MP sends a postal code to a public lookup service (see "Network use").

What is stored, and where

The app saves the following only on your device, in the operating system's app storage. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else:

  • Your first name and province or territory (from the setup screen)
  • Your study progress, practice-test history, test-day simulations and your answer preferences
  • Your settings: language, voice, daily reminder and, if you add it, your test date
  • If you use Find My MP: your federal riding and your MP's name (never your postal code)

We cannot see this data. It is deleted when you choose Settings → Reset app data or uninstall the app.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): if you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification (the Question of the Day) on your device. Reminders are created on your device and are not sent through us or any server.
  • Background audio: Listen Mode keeps playing when you lock the screen or switch apps, and shows the current question on the lock screen or in a notification so you can pause it. No audio is recorded.

The app does not use your microphone, camera, contacts, location, photos or files.

Listen Mode and read-aloud

The questions and answers are voice recordings built into the app, and they play offline. If a recording doesn't exist (for example, a newly elected official), your phone's own text-to-speech reads it on your device. Nothing you study is sent anywhere to make audio.

Test-day simulation

The test-day simulation and ID guide are practice only. They do not use the camera or the microphone, and nothing is recorded.

Network use

  • Find My MP (optional): when you type your postal code, the app sends it once over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to Represent by Open North, a free non-profit civic lookup, to find your federal riding. The app sends nothing else (no name, no device ID) and does not save the postal code. Open North's own privacy policy (opennorth.ca) applies to its service. If you'd rather not use the network, choose your riding from the built-in list instead. The setup screen works out your province from the first letter of your postal code without any network.
  • Officials list: names of current officials (for example, the Prime Minister and premiers) are built into the app. A future version may download an updated public list; that sends none of your data.
  • Ratings and sharing: if you choose to rate the app, the rating prompt is shown by Apple or Google, and what you submit goes to them under their own privacy policies. "Share with a friend" opens your phone's share sheet with a text message; you choose where it goes.

Children's privacy

The app is made for adults preparing for the citizenship test. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, and it collects no personal information at all.

Third parties

We do not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs or any third-party service that would receive your information. The only outside service is the optional Find My MP lookup described above. We do not sell or share any data.

Not affiliated with the government

Maple Civics is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) or the Government of Canada. Always confirm current test information at canada.ca.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
